FAQ

Good questions.

Where is my data stored?

Your journal is saved on your device and synced to your private cloud account, so it is the same on every device. Only you can see it. Export a full backup from Customize at any time.

Can I bring my old trades?

Yes. Export your Notion journal as CSV and import it. You match the columns once and the trades come across. Tec Journal is not affiliated with Notion.

Do I have to trade NQ and ES?

No. Instruments, models, timeframes, confluences and mistakes are all editable, so the journal fits how you trade.

Does it connect to my broker?

Not yet. You log trades yourself or import a CSV, which keeps you looking at every trade you take.

Can I journal backtests separately?

Yes. Switch between Live and Backtest and each keeps its own trades, days and stats.
