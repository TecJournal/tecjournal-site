Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Tec Journal collects, why, who handles it, and what your rights are. We keep it plain and we collect as little as we can. It follows the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Norwegian Personal Data Act.

1. Who is responsible

Who we are. Tec Journal is run by Mathias nybo, a private individual in Norway ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@tecjournal.app We are the "data controller" for the data described below.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and a password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. We cannot read it.
  • Journal content you create: trades, notes, daily analysis, checklists, settings, account labels and screenshots you upload. You decide what to enter. Please do not enter anything you do not want stored, such as brokerage login details.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and timestamps, which our hosting and authentication providers log for security and to keep the service running.
  • Email: if you contact us, we keep your message and our reply.

We do not collect payment card details (there is no paid plan yet; if that changes, payments will be handled by a payment provider and this policy will be updated). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, we do not sell your data, and we do not use your journal for advertising or to train AI models.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To create your account, store and sync your journal and let you log in: performing our agreement with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To send account emails such as confirmation and password reset: performing our agreement (art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems: our legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal duties, for example answering a lawful request: legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).

4. Who handles your data (processors)

We use these providers to run the service. They process data on our behalf under their own data-protection terms:

  • Supabase – authentication, database and file storage for your account and journal, and sending account emails.
  • Vercel – hosts the website and may log technical request data (such as IP address).
  • GitHub – hosts the downloadable desktop apps. Downloading an app means GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Namecheap – our domain name registrar. It does not see your journal.

Some of these providers are based in or may process data in the USA or other countries outside the EU/EEA. Where that happens, the transfer relies on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Where your journal lives

Your journal is stored on your own device (in your browser or desktop app) and synced to your private cloud account so it is the same on every device. Your data is protected so that other users cannot read it. Because we operate the service, we technically have administrative access to the database and will only use it to run, secure and repair the service, or when the law requires.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use only what is necessary: your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to keep you logged in, remember settings and hold your journal for offline use. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and journal until you delete your account. In the app, go to Account, then Delete account, to permanently remove your account and cloud data. Backups held by our providers may keep copies for a short time afterwards before they expire. We may keep limited records for longer if the law requires it or to handle a dispute.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to: give you access to your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to our use of it; and give you a copy in a portable format (the app also has an export feature). Where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to support@tecjournal.app and we will answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no) or your local authority.

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to each account is restricted to its owner. No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password and tell us straight away if you suspect a problem. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

Tec Journal is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, if the change is important, tell you in the app or by email.
