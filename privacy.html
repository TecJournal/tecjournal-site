Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Tac Journal is a trading journal. This page explains what we store and why. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, to create and secure your account.
  • What you put in your journal: trades, daily notes, checklists, settings, prop account details and the screenshots you add.

We do not ask for broker logins, card numbers or your identity documents. We do not run advertising or sell your data.

Where it is stored

Your journal is saved on your own device and synced to a private cloud database and file storage provided by Supabase. Access rules ensure that only your account can read your entries. Our website is hosted on Vercel.

How we use it

Only to run the journal for you: to log you in, sync your data between your devices, and send account emails such as email confirmation.

Your rights

You can export all of your data from Customize at any time. You can delete your account and all of your cloud data yourself from your account settings in the app. If you are in the EU/EEA you also have the right to access, correct and erase your data and to complain to your data protection authority.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage only to keep you logged in and to keep your journal on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
